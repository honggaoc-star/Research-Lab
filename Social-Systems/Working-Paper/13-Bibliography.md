# 13. Bibliography

To maintain consistency during the development of the manuscript, in-text citations are introduced progressively as individual chapters mature. The complete bibliography will be finalized prior to journal submission.

The reference list will include the principal works supporting the conceptual framework, mathematical formalization, empirical methodology, and validation strategy presented throughout this paper.
