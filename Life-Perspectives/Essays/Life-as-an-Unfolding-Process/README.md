# Life as an Unfolding Process: An Essay

**Version:** 1.0<br>
**Date:** September 2026<br>
**Author:** Honggao Cao

*Life as an Unfolding Process: An Essay* asks readers to value and enjoy life while it is being lived, tracing that concern through ordinary activities, changing life stages, consequential choices, work, saving, giving, retirement, and later beginnings. It argues for pursuing what matters without making achievement the condition on which living becomes worthwhile.
