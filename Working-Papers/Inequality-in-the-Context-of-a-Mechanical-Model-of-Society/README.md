# Inequality in the Context of a Mechanical Model of Society

*Nature, Nurture, and the Separation of Mechanism from Correction*

**Version:** 1.0<br>
**Date:** October 2026<br>
**Author:** Honggao Cao

This paper helps readers understand why inequality can rise even when correction makes a substantial difference. A mechanical baseline follows how heterogeneous endowments and accumulated outcomes reinforce one another. A budget-balanced transfer example distinguishes what correction achieves relative to that baseline from the further improvement attainable under specified interventions. An efficient frontier organizes the latter comparison for a stated mechanism, correction technology, timing, and horizon.
