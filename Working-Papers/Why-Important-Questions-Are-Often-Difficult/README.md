# Why Important Questions Are Often Difficult

*Significance, Persistence, and the Composition of Unresolved Inquiry*

**Version:** 1.1<br>
**Date:** September 2026<br>
**Author:** Honggao Cao

This paper examines how significant and difficult questions become prominent among inquiries that remain active and unresolved. It separates resolution from disengagement, uses a competing-risks representation to explain how the surviving population can become enriched in both significance and difficulty, and identifies the additional conditions required for those dimensions to become statistically associated.
