# Luck in a Puzzling World: An Essay

**Version:** 1.1  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Author:** Honggao Cao

*Luck in a Puzzling World: An Essay* examines what realized success and failure can legitimately tell us about strategy, capability, merit, and luck. It separates what people choose from what could happen, what actually happens, and the outcomes that follow, then traces successful outcomes into attribution, social learning, and feedback.

The essay is a reader-facing synthesis produced from the broader Research on Luck project. It is not a replacement for the underlying phase-specific research record.
