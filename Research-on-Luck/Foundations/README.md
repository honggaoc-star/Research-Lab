# Foundations

## Overview

The **Foundations** directory establishes the conceptual basis of the **Research on Luck** project.

Scientific research begins with clearly defining its subject. Before developing mathematical models, empirical methods, or practical applications, it is necessary to establish a consistent vocabulary and a coherent conceptual framework. The documents in this directory address that objective.

Rather than proposing complete theories, the papers in **Foundations** identify the fundamental concepts, distinctions, assumptions, and questions that guide subsequent stages of the research.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of this directory are to:

* Develop rigorous definitions of core concepts.
* Clarify terminology used throughout the project.
* Distinguish related but different concepts.
* Review major conceptual perspectives from existing literature.
* Identify unresolved questions that motivate further research.

The emphasis is on **conceptual clarity** rather than mathematical formalization or empirical validation.

---

# Scope

Topics appropriate for this directory include:

* Defining luck
* Taxonomies of luck
* Luck versus randomness
* Luck versus risk and uncertainty
* Sources and origins of luck
* Fundamental conceptual distinctions
* Foundational research questions

Topics such as mathematical models, statistical methods, simulations, or domain-specific applications belong in other sections of the repository.

# Relationship to Other Directories

The research workflow follows a progressive structure.

```text
Foundations
      ↓
Literature
      ↓
Formalization
      ↓
Measurement
      ↓
Applications
```

Each stage builds upon the conceptual framework established in **Foundations**.

---

# Guiding Principles

Documents in this directory should adhere to the following principles.

* Prioritize conceptual precision over breadth.
* Clearly distinguish definitions from hypotheses.
* Separate descriptive statements from normative claims.
* Minimize mathematical formalism unless essential for clarification.
* Identify assumptions explicitly.
* Maintain consistency with terminology used throughout the repository.

---

# Long-Term Goal

The long-term goal of **Foundations** is to provide a stable conceptual framework upon which the remainder of the **Research on Luck** project can be built.

As the project evolves, definitions may be refined and new concepts introduced. However, revisions should preserve internal consistency and improve conceptual clarity rather than simply expand the scope of discussion.
