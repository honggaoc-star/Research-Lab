## Recognition-Field Adjudication

### Apparent disagreement

For each candidate:

- Alpha coded recognition as **supported as historical recognition; materially undermined as an evidentiary claim**.
- Coder B coded cumulative recognition as **contested**, while explicitly stating that the original collaboration's historical recognition remained supported.

The difference concerns what the cumulative Recognition row should represent:

1. whether the focal actor historically interpreted the candidate as actionable; or
2. whether the recognized claim remained evidentially credible at the Pass B horizon.

### Applicable rule

The frozen recognition construct concerns the actor's dated interpretation of a possibility as actionable. Later evidence may change the appraisal of that recognition and support Category 8, but it does not retroactively erase or contest the occurrence of the recognition act unless new evidence disputes whether that act occurred.

The Pass B packet separately asks:

- whether Pass B altered the interpretation of Pass A recognition; and
- whether premature or false recognition is supported.

Those fields carry the later evidentiary appraisal.

### Adjudicated coding

For CF-01A, CF-01B, and CF-01C:

- **Recognition:** Supported as a historical stage.
- **Later evidentiary standing of the recognized target:** Contested and materially undermined.
- **Final category:** 8 — Premature recognition; categorical falsehood not established.

No coder record is rewritten. This adjudication resolves the status-allocation ambiguity prospectively.

## Methodological Findings

CF-01 supplies a contrasting outcome to FLEM-01 while preserving the separability of the core stages:

- recognition and pursuit can occur without realization;
- later corrective evidence can alter the appraisal of recognition without erasing the historical recognition act;
- nonrealization does not imply foreclosure;
- negative replication and authoritative rejection must remain distinct from categorical disproof;
- nested scientific, causal, and development claims require separate criteria;
- and Category 8 is usable when it is qualified as premature recognition rather than treated as a claim of fraud or permanent falsehood.

The repeated Recognition-row ambiguity suggests one prospective Version 0.3 clarification: later evidence should not change the historical Recognition stage code unless it disputes whether recognition occurred. A separate field should carry the later validity or credibility status of the recognized target.

## CF-01 Conclusion

CF-01 is complete as a Tier 2 controlled replication.

The episode provides provisional cross-case evidence that the Version 0.2 framework can distinguish:

- recognition from validity;
- pursuit from realization;
- nonrealization from foreclosure;
- and premature recognition from categorical falsehood.

It does not estimate the prevalence or causal effect of recognition and does not validate Proposition 4. The next program decision is whether the remaining heterogeneous pilot cases are still necessary before a within-domain Stage 2 design.
