## Instrument Revisions Before Pass B

The comparison supports five bounded revisions:

1. Define **potential** independently from access and from knowledge of later feasibility.
2. State whether a conjunctive candidate such as 01D requires evidence for every component.
3. Define candidate-specific **realization thresholds** before coding.
4. Split Category 5 into separate delayed-pursuit and partial-pursuit states, or remove “partial” when pursuit is already coded supported.
5. Define **foreclosure** as an affirmative transition requiring evidence; continuing uncertainty or missing capability is not foreclosure.

These revisions should govern Pass B prospectively. They must not be used to rewrite the locked Pass A records.

## Construct-Validation Finding

Within this single bounded episode, recognition was separated consistently from access, pursuit, realization, and higher-level organizational inference. The strongest result is not the percentage alone; it is the convergence on different recognition targets:

- recognition of a research program for characterization;
- recognition and realization of a laboratory isolation tool;
- recognition and pursuit of local therapeutic or antiseptic use without demonstrated efficacy; and
- no demonstrated recognition of the later bundled systemic-medicine opportunity.

This provisionally supports the discriminant validity of candidate-specific recognition coding. It does not validate Proposition 4, establish historical prevalence, or show that recognition causes realization.

## Pass B Release Decision

**Pass A is closed and Pass B may be released after its boundary, outcome horizon, realization criteria, and source list are frozen.**

Pass B must not become an unrestricted history of penicillin. It should add only the evidence needed to evaluate later pursuit, realization, foreclosure, system-level transitions, and hindsight-sensitive revisions for the four fixed opportunities. Every change from Pass A must be entered in a revision log with the new evidence ID and a stated reason.
