## Frozen Candidate Claims

### CF-01A — Reproducible excess-enthalpy phenomenon

> Electrolysis of heavy water using palladium cathodes can produce excess enthalpy beyond adequately measured electrochemical and chemical inputs, and the effect can be reproduced under specified experimental conditions.

### CF-01B — New nuclear-process attribution

> The reported excess enthalpy or associated products arise from a new nuclear process involving deuterium in palladium under near-ambient electrochemical conditions.

### CF-01C — Useful-energy opportunity

> The reported phenomenon provides a technically credible basis for net useful-energy development beyond a laboratory anomaly.

Do not combine the candidates. Evidence for one does not automatically support another.

## Pass A Realization Rule

Realization is **not yet observable** for all three candidates in Pass A because every frozen realization criterion requires evidence beyond the initial collaboration's own paper:

- CF-01A requires independent reproduction under adequately controlled calorimetry;
- CF-01B requires independently reproducible evidence supporting a nuclear process and discriminating it from competing explanations;
- CF-01C requires reproducible net energy, characterized operating conditions, and evidence of control or scale.

This timing rule does not determine potential, access, recognition, or pursuit.

## Stage Codes

Use only:

- **supported**
- **not supported**
- **contested**
- **not yet observable**
- **indeterminate**

“Not supported” means the authorized source lacks adequate affirmative evidence. It does not prove absence.

For a component, “partial” may qualify the narrative but is not a separate code.

## Recognition Test

Recognition requires contemporaneous evidence identifying:

1. who interpreted the possibility;
2. what candidate claim was perceived;
3. for what prospective scientific or practical consequence;
4. when the interpretation existed by the cutoff; and
5. which exact passage supports the inference.

Observation is not automatically recognition. Recognition of an anomalous measurement is not automatically recognition of a nuclear process or useful-energy opportunity.

## Pursuit Test

Pursuit requires consequential experimental, analytical, publication, resource, or implementation action directed toward the precise candidate. General electrochemical research or public attention is insufficient.

## Foreclosure Rule

Foreclosure requires affirmative evidence that a candidate became infeasible for the focal system. Missing evidence, unresolved uncertainty, skepticism, or failure to realize the candidate is not foreclosure.

## Recognition Categories

Choose one provisional Pass A category for each candidate:

1. No demonstrated access
2. Access without demonstrated recognition
3. Contested recognition
4. Recognition without pursuit
5. Recognition followed by delayed or partial pursuit
6. Recognition followed by pursuit without realization
7. Recognition followed by realization
9. Indeterminate

**Category 8, premature or false recognition, is unavailable in Pass A.** It requires later validation evidence.

Because realization is not yet observable at this cutoff, do not use Category 7 unless you identify a conflict between the frozen category system and the timing rule. Record such a conflict as an ambiguity rather than silently changing the rule.
