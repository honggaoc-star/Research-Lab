## Recognition Rule

A positive recognition code requires evidence of:

1. a relevant actor;
2. interpretation of a specified possibility;
3. a prospective purpose or consequence;
4. timing no later than the cutoff;
5. and recoverable support in the supplied source.

Observation, naming, experimentation, publication, and success are analytically distinct. Explain which of them carries the recognition inference.

## Pursuit Rule

Distinguish:

- investigation or characterization;
- laboratory application;
- local therapeutic experimentation;
- and drug-development commitment.

Do not infer budget, staffing, organizational authorization, purification capacity, or production capability unless the source establishes it.
