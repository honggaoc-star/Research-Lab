# Figures

## Purpose

The **Figures/** directory contains the repository's shared conceptual and visual assets.

Unlike figures embedded within individual research papers, the figures in this directory are **repository-wide references**. They communicate the conceptual architecture, research framework, and major theoretical relationships that span multiple sections of the repository.

These figures serve as common reference points for the research documents and should be cited rather than duplicated whenever possible.

---

## Design Philosophy

Figures are intended to improve clarity rather than replace explanation.

Each figure should:

* communicate a single primary idea;
* complement, rather than duplicate, the accompanying text;
* remain conceptually consistent with the repository architecture;
* evolve only when the underlying theory or framework changes.

Whenever practical, figures should be reusable across multiple documents.

---

## Organization

Figures are numbered sequentially in the order that they are introduced into the repository.

Each figure normally consists of:

* a **PNG** file for visualization; and
* a corresponding **Markdown** file describing the figure, its purpose, interpretation, and relationship to the research framework.

## Current Figures

### Figure 01 — Conceptual Architecture of Adaptive Systems Under Uncertainty

The master architectural figure for the repository.

It illustrates the conceptual framework underlying **Research on Luck**, beginning with the working primitives:

* System
* Environment
* Interaction
* Uncertainty

and progressing through:

* Derived Conditions
* System Capabilities
* Adaptive Processes
* Long-Term Effects
* Outcome Realization
* Outcome Attribution

Figure 01 serves as the conceptual blueprint for the repository. The major research directories develop, formalize, operationalize, test, and synthesize different portions of this architecture.

---

## Relationship to the Repository

The figures complement the primary research directories:

* **Foundations** defines the canonical concepts represented in the figures.
* **Formalization** specifies the mechanisms and relationships illustrated by the figures.
* **Measurement** operationalizes the constructs shown in the figures.
* **Empirical Studies** evaluate the framework across multiple domains.
* **Comparative Studies** identify recurring patterns and relationships.
* **General Theory** synthesizes the validated relationships into a coherent theoretical framework.

---

## Relationship to the Design Folder

The **Design/** folder documents **how** the conceptual architecture was developed.

The **Figures/** folder presents the **finalized visual representation** of that architecture.

In other words:

* **Design/** records the evolution of the ideas.
* **Figures/** communicates the mature framework.

This separation preserves the design history while keeping the conceptual assets concise, stable, and reusable throughout the repository.
