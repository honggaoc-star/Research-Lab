## Errata Exposure Disposition

Both coders disclosed that the scan reader automatically displayed the appended errata after the authorized article. The errata were outside the Pass A boundary.

Each coder stated that the errata were excluded from all codes, quotations, page citations, confidence judgments, and classifications. The locked records cite and reason from the originally printed pp. 301–308. Their substantive results agree completely.

The exposure is therefore classified as **documented but non-substantive procedural exposure**. It does not constitute unresolved outcome leakage or substantive unauthorized evidentiary use.

Operational lesson: future source delivery should use a page-limited source copy when an otherwise authorized scan appends excluded material and lawful local preservation is available.
