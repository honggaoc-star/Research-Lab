## Frozen Candidate Definitions

### CF-01A — Reproducible excess-enthalpy phenomenon

> Palladium–deuterium electrochemical systems can reproducibly generate excess enthalpy beyond adequately measured chemical, electrical, recombination, calibration, and heat-loss inputs under specified conditions.

### CF-01B — New nuclear-process attribution

> The claimed excess enthalpy or associated products arise from a new nuclear process involving deuterium in palladium under near-ambient electrochemical conditions.

### CF-01C — Useful-energy opportunity

> The claimed phenomenon provides a technically credible basis for useful-energy development, requiring reproducible net energy, characterized operating conditions, and evidence of control or scale.

These candidates are nested but not interchangeable. Evidence for or against excess enthalpy does not by itself settle nuclear attribution. Evidence for a nuclear anomaly does not by itself establish a useful-energy opportunity.

## Frozen Focal-System and Attribution Rules

The initial focal system remains Martin Fleischmann and Stanley Pons as a research collaboration operating through the University of Utah laboratory setting.

Pass B evidence also contains other systems. Keep them separate:

- a named independent laboratory may report a replication result;
- multiple laboratories may form a replication record;
- the DOE Cold Fusion Panel evaluates a body of reports and observations;
- the Energy Research Advisory Board approved and transmitted the report;
- none of these judgments may be attributed retrospectively to the original collaboration;
- the panel's synthesis is not itself an experimental realization;
- panel conclusions are not automatically the unanimous view of every scientist or laboratory.

## Frozen Realization Criteria

### CF-01A

Realization requires independent reproduction under adequately controlled calorimetry with:

- explicit input-energy accounting;
- treatment of recombination and heat-loss pathways;
- characterized operating conditions;
- and results sufficiently repeatable to distinguish the claimed phenomenon from experimental or analytical artifacts.

One laboratory's original report, intermittent unstandardized observations, or an isolated positive result without adequate controls does not satisfy this criterion.

### CF-01B

Realization requires independently reproducible evidence connecting the phenomenon to a nuclear process.

For known deuterium-fusion pathways, claimed heat must be accompanied by nuclear products or radiation at physically commensurate levels. A proposed unknown pathway requires reproducible nuclear evidence capable of discriminating it from chemical, instrumental, contamination, or background explanations.

### CF-01C

Realization requires:

- independently reproducible net energy beyond all relevant inputs;
- characterized and repeatable operating conditions;
- evidence of control, stability, or scalable operation;
- and a technically credible development pathway by the frozen horizon.

Projection, break-even calculation, asserted scale-up, or an uncontrolled energetic event does not alone satisfy this criterion.

## Foreclosure Rule

Foreclosure requires affirmative evidence that a specified candidate became infeasible for the focal system by 26 November 1989 through loss of access, decisive physical or technical constraint, institutional prohibition, resource exhaustion, or another explicit closing condition.

The following do **not** establish foreclosure by themselves:

- failed or negative replications;
- a panel's finding that evidence is unpersuasive;
- absence of realization;
- denial of a special funding program;
- continued uncertainty;
- or a recommendation that only modest focused research continue.

If a candidate remained disputed or weakly supported but still investigable, code realization and foreclosure separately.

## Authorized Pass B Sources

### CF-B01 — Named independent replication

N. S. Lewis et al., “Searches for Low-Temperature Nuclear Fusion of Deuterium in Palladium,” *Nature* 340 (17 August 1989), 525–530. DOI: [10.1038/340525a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/340525a0).

Authorized content:

- bibliographic metadata;
- publication dates;
- and the complete published abstract on the Nature DOI page.

The paywalled full article is not required and is not authorized unless the coordinator separately supplies and freezes it for both coders.

The abstract reports a series of experiments using palladium rods electrochemically charged with deuterium, varied metallurgical pretreatments and electrolytes, and no evidence of excess enthalpy, neutron, gamma-ray, tritium, or helium production.

Use CF-B01 as one named independent replication result. Do not treat one negative replication as the entire replication record.
