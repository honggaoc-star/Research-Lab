## Status

**FLEM-01-B Evidence Inventory, Version 1.0-RC1 — 20 July 2026**

This inventory supports the [bounded Pass B coder packet](07-Fleming-Penicillin-Bounded-Pass-B-Coder-Packet.md). It is an auditable release candidate, not a final coding result. It records what each included source can and cannot establish before the packet is frozen.

## Competing and Limiting Evidence Inventory

| Constraint | Evidence IDs | Required coding caution |
|---|---|---|
| Impurity and incomplete purification | F-B1-02–03; F-B2-04 | Do not equate active powder or “therapeutic penicillin” with chemically pure penicillin |
| Toxicity and pyrogen | F-B1-03; F-B2-04 | Separate toxicity of penicillin from impurities; retain the rat kidney observation and human pyrogen response |
| Stability | F-B1-02; F-B2-02, F-B2-04–05 | Do not generalize a stored standard's stability to all preparations, routes, or production stages |
| Dose and administration uncertainty | F-B1-04–05; F-B2-05, F-B2-08–09 | Clinical response does not establish optimized dosage, route, or durability |
| Small and uncontrolled clinical evidence | F-B2-06–08 | Code observed response within bounds; do not infer comparative efficacy or population effect |
| Supply and contamination constraints | F-B2-03, F-B2-09 | Laboratory production does not satisfy operational scale |
| Aggregate production attribution | F-B3-01–03; F-B4-01–02 | Attribute scale to the production network or WPB-reported system, not Fleming or the Oxford team alone |
| Preliminary/incomplete totals | F-B3-02; F-B4-01–02 | Distinguish plans from realized output and Army figures from total production |

## Outcome-Leakage Audit

The core source set necessarily contains outcome evidence because Pass B tests full-trajectory coding. Leakage control therefore changes form:

- Later outcomes may revise pursuit, realization, foreclosure, and system succession.
- Later outcomes may not enlarge Fleming's 1929 recognition target.
- The 1940 and 1941 authors' summaries of Fleming are not independent evidence of his private or organizational recognition.
- Government production totals do not establish earlier technical feasibility or actor-level recognition.
- Retrospective celebration, Nobel attribution, later global use, and antibiotic-era narratives remain excluded.
