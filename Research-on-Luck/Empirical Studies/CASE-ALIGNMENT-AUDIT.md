# Empirical Case Architecture-Alignment Audit

## Status

**Preliminary revision map**

This audit classifies the current Empirical Studies case and thematic files before case-level rewriting.

It is based on:

- full-text structural scanning;
- section and mechanism coverage;
- the updated domain READMEs;
- the five domain syntheses;
- and the Foundations, Formalization, and Measurement architecture.

It is not a final evidence grade. A missing heading does not prove that a concept is absent, and keyword presence does not prove that a mechanism is supported.

No case is classified as **reassessment required** solely from structural evidence. That category requires substantive source and argument review.

---

## Classification Rules

### Aligned

The file already includes most of the following:

- initial conditions;
- uncertainty or contingency;
- opportunity or constraint;
- recognition or response;
- path dependence or trajectory;
- feedback or institutional mediation;
- alternative explanations or limitations;
- and explicit lessons or framework evaluation.

Only ordinary editorial maintenance is indicated.

### Alignment by cross-reference

The substantive narrative is broadly compatible with the architecture, but the file would benefit from a short cross-reference or audit note clarifying:

- focal system and level;
- exposure;
- opportunity stages;
- capability–outcome separation;
- unrealized alternatives;
- or evidence status.

### Targeted revision

A material component is structurally weak or absent, such as:

- ex ante uncertainty;
- system boundary;
- interaction and exposure;
- mechanism evidence;
- counterfactual feasibility;
- survivorship;
- or competing explanations.

The case should be revised selectively rather than rewritten from scratch.

### Reassessment required

Reserved for cases whose central argument depends on circular capability inference, unsupported determinism, outcome-only narration, or evidence too weak for the claim. None is assigned at this preliminary stage.
